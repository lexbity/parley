# TD-03 · Bounded daily queue and the missing budget tests

Slice evidence for TD-001 FR-2 / NFR-1 / NFR-2. Recorded against `backend`
`4605925` with the TD-03 edits applied.

## Goal

The legacy daily queue returns bounded, counted projections within budget; the
sheet route gains its wall-clock budget test; Daily.vue makes the bound
visible; the 700-character single-statement fetch-all is replaced by a
readable, bound-parameterised construction.

## What changed

- `crates/parley-service/src/queueing.rs`: the monolithic `fetch_all` is gone.
  A shared `DAILY_QUEUE_BASE_SQL` CTE computes the blocked/actionable
  classification once (the owner's `max_touches` fence is `?1`), and two
  statements drive the response:
  1. one `COUNT` projection returning `total_actionable`, `total_blocked` and
     `ready_count` over the full filtered set;
  2. one windowed page (`row_number() OVER (PARTITION BY kind ORDER BY
     opportunity_id, party_id)`, `rn <= 200`) returning at most 200 rows per
     class in the legacy global ordering.
  Both statements are assembled with `sqlx::QueryBuilder` from module-owned
  literals; no caller text is interpolated. Per-row thread pick and shortlist
  lookup stay indexed probes, now for at most 400 rows instead of all rows.
- Wire shape is additive only: `event_cursor`, `actionable`, `blocked`,
  `suggested_send_count` are unchanged; `total_actionable` and `total_blocked`
  are required new fields. `suggested_send_count` is computed from the full
  `ready_count` so capping cannot undercount it.
- `contract/openapi.yaml`: `QueueSnapshot` gains the two required integer
  totals and `maxItems: 200` on both arrays; `getQueue` fixtures are now
  schema-pinned (valid snapshot, error envelope) and `generated.ts` was
  regenerated from the contract.
- `crates/parley-service/tests/s10.rs`: the 5,000-party benchmark now asserts
  capped arrays plus full totals, and adds a second 20-sample benchmark for
  the UI-rendered `view=list&limit=50` sheet route. New
  `s10_daily_queue_bounded_and_counts_at_scale` seeds 5,000 actionable and 300
  blocked pairs, asserts 200/200 arrays, 5,000/300 totals, the raw payload
  budget, and a 20-sample warm p95.
- `web/src/features/queue/Daily.vue`: renders `+N more not shown` from the
  server's authoritative `deferred_count` (the hidden actionable count), plus
  a vitest case covering capped / within-cap / zero states.

## Review note: specification vs. this tree

The spec states that `Daily.vue` consumes the legacy `actionable`/`blocked`
queue response. In this tree it consumes the richer `view=daily` snapshot
(`as_of`, `pack_id`, `actionable_count`, `deferred_count`, `items`) through
`client.ts::getDailyQueue`; the Rust service currently serves the legacy shape
for that view while the mock serves the rich shape. TD-03 keeps both wire
shapes unchanged and makes the bound visible in the surface that actually
renders it (`deferred_count`), rather than rewriting the daily-view contract
mid-slice. The legacy shape's new totals are proven by the Rust suite and the
contract fixture. Unifying the two daily shapes remains a separate surface
decision and is not silently assumed here.

The spec's Q2 describes the existing daily ordering as `due_at ASC`; the
shipped implementation orders by `(opportunity_id, party_id)`. The bounded
page preserves the shipped ordering so no consumer sees reordered items.
