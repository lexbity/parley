# AR04 evidence — scoped Interviewer and Brief work across navigation

## Slice exit criteria

- **One typed intake per role.** `application/interview.rs` owns every
  Interviewer owner-turn start; `application/brief.rs` owns both Briefer
  starts (`Generate` and `ChatTurn`). The HTTP handlers no longer resolve
  scope, prompts or jobs, and the Coach-answer flow enqueues through the same
  Interviewer intake (`ar04_coach_answer_reaches_interviewer_candidate_review`).
- **Chat jobs bind their chat and freeze their prompt/profile at admission.**
  `ar04_typed_intake_binds_chat_job_and_profile_at_admission` asserts the new
  `durable_job.chat_id` column, the frozen dependency digest and the scope
  revision; chat summaries and detail reads now use the indexed column instead
  of scanning job JSON.
- **Late or cancelled results cannot overwrite a newer owner turn.**
  `ar04_late_chat_result_cannot_publish_after_new_turn_or_cancel` holds the
  provider for the first job, writes a second owner turn, then releases the
  stalled response: the first job fails `STALE_INPUT`, never publishes an
  agent turn or candidate, and only the second job's result becomes visible.
- **Owner and registry Brief starts share one scoped intake.**
  `ar04_owner_and_registry_brief_share_scoped_intake` proves one durable job
  per intent, exact replay receipts, and the same binding columns as the
  delegated `brief.start` command path.
- **Browser flows survive reload and keep scoped review state.**
  `ar04_interviewer_resumes_after_reload` and
  `ar04_brief_review_returns_to_matching_context` exercise the packaged UI on
  both browser projects; chat polling is replaced by the shared
  `observeRoleJob` reconciler in `web/src/app/inference/activity.ts`, and the
  sessionStorage brief hand-off is gone in favor of a typed route query.
