# R02 evidence — eligibility, deliveries and release recheck

R02 extends the resource board with the lifecycle and delivery-distinction
behavior of the amendment §7:

- Board summaries now carry the deterministic missing-typed-field list
  (`gaps`) per subtype; eligibility is derived from it. An unfinished proof
  placed in Available remains ineligible and visibly reports its gaps.
- `GET /resource-cards/{id}/deliveries` returns derived delivery rows: sends
  whose delivered material version contains the resource or one of its bound
  files. Drafting-only dependencies never appear; repeat sends are separate
  rows. Each row carries date, recipient, opportunity and exact material
  version.
- Editing a resource that a material version depends on commits an
  `affected_versions` list and an observable
  `material.release_recheck_required` event, so future approvals/releases are
  rechecked against the fresh dependency revision while historical approvals
  and releases remain immutable.
- Frontend: inspector shows the eligibility/gap callout and delivery records
  with a route to Record delivery (Output); staged uploads that fail can be
  retried in place; the workspace reacts to epoch-changing admin operations by
  refreshing (or discarding a dirty draft after confirmation).

Verified by `tests/r02.rs` (3 cases) and the extended workspace component
spec; mock parity added for the new endpoint and summary fields.
